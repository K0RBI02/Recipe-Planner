# Changelog

## 1.0.2 (Minecraft 26.3)

- Items inside shulker boxes and bundles are counted too (in your inventory and in the open chest)

## 1.0.1 (Minecraft 26.3)

- The list is now always the compact column: item icons with the number on them, like stack sizes
- The list no longer reaches below the inventory window; long lists scroll instead
- Tooltips say "1 stack" instead of "1 stacks"

## 1.0.0 (Minecraft 26.3)

First release.

- Right-click recipes in the recipe book to add them to your crafting plan (Shift: +10)
- Compact list next to inventory, crafting table and chests
- Shows missing ingredients, counting your inventory and the open chest
- Already crafted items are subtracted automatically
- Compact mode when there is little room on screen
- English and German translations
